Dorian Vex

Assistant Professor of Computational Biology

Department of Computational Intelligence, Averton Institute of Technology

Research interests: protein structure prediction, generative models, computational biology.

Professor Vex develops generative deep learning methods for protein design, with a focus on diffusion models that propose novel enzymes and binders which are then validated in high-throughput wet-lab screens.
