Mara Quintrell

Associate Professor of Machine Learning

Department of Computational Intelligence, Averton Institute of Technology

Research interests: reinforcement learning, robot learning, embodied agents.

Professor Quintrell studies how embodied agents acquire general skills from sparse feedback, combining model-based reinforcement learning with large pretrained world models to control real robot fleets in warehouses and hospitals.
